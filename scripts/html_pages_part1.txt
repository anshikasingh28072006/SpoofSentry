    <h2 class="main-heading">ABSTRACT</h2>
    
    <p style="margin-top: 16px; line-height: 1.7; text-align: justify;">
      Email remains the foundational communication protocol of global commerce, academia, and enterprise infrastructure, transmitting upwards of 340 billion messages daily. However, the foundational protocol underpinning email transmission—the Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP, RFC 821/5321)—was designed in 1982 without built-in sender verification mechanisms. This architectural deficiency enables malicious adversaries to execute Email Spoofing, forging header fields to masquerade as trusted corporate executives, financial institutions, or university officials. According to the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3), Business Email Compromise (BEC) and email spoofing account for over $2.9 billion in annual financial losses.
    </p>

    <p style="margin-top: 12px; line-height: 1.7; text-align: justify;">
      Existing email security solutions, such as enterprise secure email gateways (SEGs), operate as costly, opaque black-boxes that quarantine messages without educating the recipient on why an email failed authentication. To bridge this critical usability and security divide, we have engineered SpoofSentry—an intelligent, browser-native, privacy-centric email spoofing and threat forensics web application. Built with React 19, TypeScript, and modern Web APIs, SpoofSentry provides transparent, explainable forensic evaluation without routing confidential internal emails through external third-party servers.
    </p>

    <p style="margin-top: 12px; line-height: 1.7; text-align: justify;">
      SpoofSentry implements four multi-modal ingestion vectors: raw RFC 5322 header pasting, drag-and-drop .EML file parsing, mobile email screenshot OCR via Gemini Vision Multimodal AI, and direct Gmail OAuth inbox synchronization. The system parses folded headers, extracts Mail Transfer Agent (MTA) hops, queries authoritative DNS TXT records for SPF (RFC 7208), DKIM (RFC 6376), and DMARC (RFC 7489) policies, calculates hop transit latency anomalies, evaluates content urgency heuristics, and generates an explainable composite threat score (0-100) with actionable defense recommendations.
    </p>
